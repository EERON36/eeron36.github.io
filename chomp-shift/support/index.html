Chomp Shift is in development. It is not yet available on the App Store.

How do I play?

Swipe left or right to change lanes and swipe up to jump. Collect food and coins, dodge obstacles, and finish missions to raise your score multiplier.

Can I play without an internet connection?

The installed game can run offline. Ads and optional video rewards need a connection and may sometimes be unavailable.

Where is my progress saved?

Your scores, coins, hats, missions and settings are saved on your device. There is no game account or developer-operated cloud save. Clearing app data or deleting the app can erase this progress.

How do I change ad privacy choices?

Where required, open Settings in the game and choose Ad privacy choices. You can review your choices without watching an ad.

Something went wrong

Email us with your iPhone model, iOS version, game version and what happened. A screenshot can help. Please leave out passwords, payment details and other sensitive information.

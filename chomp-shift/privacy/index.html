Chomp Shift

Privacy policy

Effective 8 October 2026

Chomp Shift is developed by EERON36 under the Low Lumen brand. This policy covers the game’s current development build and this website. For questions or privacy requests, contact lowlumen.support@gmail.com.

At a glance: game progress stays on your device. There is no game account. The iPhone build includes Google’s advertising and consent services, which can process data even when ads are used for testing.

Game progress and settings

The game stores scores, run counts, coins, hats, mission progress, daily gift status, and sound, vibration and reduced-motion preferences in local device storage. This lets the game remember your progress and choices. The game does not send this save data to a developer-operated server.

There is no account registration, developer-operated cloud save, separate developer analytics service or in-app purchase system in this build.

Advertising and Google services

The iPhone build uses Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform to manage ads and privacy choices. The current build requests Google demo ads in test mode and requests non-personalized ads. The browser game does not use this native ad integration.

Google’s advertising software may process IP addresses (including approximate location inferred from them), device identifiers, crash and performance information, ad impressions, and interactions such as video views. These can be used for ad delivery and measurement, diagnostics, analytics and fraud prevention. Non-personalized ads still involve data processing.

Google receives data directly through its services. See Google’s Privacy Policy and its Mobile Ads data disclosure for further information, including processing, retention and international transfers.

Your advertising choices

Where applicable, Google presents a consent message before ad requests are permitted. The game requests ads only when Google’s consent service reports that requests are allowed. Where required, Settings → Ad privacy choices lets you review and change your choices. If ads cannot be requested, the game remains playable.

This build does not request Apple’s permission for tracking across other companies’ apps and websites. Watching a rewarded ad is optional; it is not a requirement to play.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use this to respond, investigate problems and handle privacy requests. Our email provider also processes the correspondence. Support messages are kept only as long as needed for those purposes or to meet applicable obligations. Please avoid sending sensitive information.

Retention and your rights

Local progress remains on your device until the app’s data is cleared or removed. Device backups may retain copies under your backup settings. We cannot retrieve or delete your local save remotely. Data processed by Google is subject to its policies and your applicable choices.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, restrict processing, object, withdraw consent, or receive a copy of certain data. Contact us to make a request concerning data we hold. You may also contact your local data protection authority; in Sweden this is IMY.

Children

Chomp Shift is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal information. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us so we can address it.

This website

The website uses no developer-added analytics, advertising scripts or cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. External links lead to services with their own privacy practices.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy when the game’s data practices change, including before switching to live advertising or adding new services. The effective date above identifies the current version.
